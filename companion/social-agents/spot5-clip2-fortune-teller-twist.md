  deadpan tech. The fortune teller doesn't read the crystal ball: she pulls out
  her OWN phone and reads the answer from the app. The buzz line writes itself:
  **"even the fortune teller uses it."** Hashtag-ready, screenshot-ready.
- **Continuity**: same room, same candles, same two characters as clip 1 — P'Fon
  face refs attached, same fortune-teller description.
- **Model**: Seedance 2.0 · **Format**: 9:16 · 8 s · continuous shot
- **Grade**: same candlelit mystical grade — do NOT brighten it; the comedy comes

- **Post-production**: super + end card below; the mystical drone SCRATCHES to a
