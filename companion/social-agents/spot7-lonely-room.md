# Agent social spot 7 — "The Lonely Room" (absurd personification, 3×8 s)

## The register — the third pillar of legendary Thai advertising

After comedy-parody (spots 1–5) and sadvertising (spot 6), the remaining register
that made Thai ads world-famous is **absurd personification played dead straight**:
Chaindrite's actors in full-size insect costumes, Sylvania's ghost grandma — give
the invisible problem a body and a heart, then have everyone treat it as
completely normal. Extracted mechanics:

1. **A ridiculous costume, a sincere soul** — the absurdity is visual, the emotion
   is real; that collision is the buzz.
2. **Humans stay deadpan** — nobody acknowledges the weirdness; the agent treats
   the costumed room like a colleague.
3. **The problem becomes a character you root for** — the empty unit isn't a
   statistic, it's a lonely guy waiting for someone to come home.
4. **Meme-ability** — the costume is a screenshot, the name is a hashtag:
   **ห้องเหงา** ("the lonely room").
5. **A warm resolution** — Thai absurdity almost always lands on belonging, not
   mockery (the sadvertising DNA runs under the joke).

Adapted to Nivas: a stale listing ("ห้องค้าง") personified. The promise underneath:
with real-time stock and fast matching, no room stays lonely for long.

## Clip 1 — alone (played dead straight)

```
Vertical 9:16, single continuous 8-second shot, static camera with a slow subtle push-in, photorealistic, melancholic muted grade like a sad Thai drama, soft grey window light.

An empty Bangkok condo unit. Sitting alone on the bare floor: a man in a soft padded full-body costume shaped like a miniature condo room: grey fabric walls, a small fabric window on his chest, a tiny fabric door flap, dusty cobwebs on his shoulders. He sadly waters a completely dead plant, sighs, looks at the front door, then flips a page of a wall calendar hanging beside him — clearly not the first page he has flipped. His expressive face: quiet loneliness, played completely straight, no comedy mugging. Dust motes drift in the pale light. No one speaks on camera. No text, no logos.
```

## Clip 2 — someone's at the door

```
Vertical 9:16, single continuous 8-second shot, handheld with gentle energy, photorealistic, bright Thai TV commercial colors, warm daylight flooding in. CHARACTER REFERENCE: the agent is the same woman as in the attached photos. SPOKEN DIALOGUE — the agent says exactly "ห้องนี้… ดูแลตัวเองดีมากค่ะ" and nothing else; no one else speaks.

The condo unit's front door swings open: a young Thai woman agent in a light blazer leads in a young Thai couple. The man in a soft padded full-body costume shaped like a miniature condo room (grey fabric walls, small fabric window on his chest, tiny fabric door flap, cobwebs on his shoulders) panics with joy — he frantically brushes the cobwebs off his shoulders, straightens his little fabric window, sucks in his belly and strikes a proud "beautiful room" pose by the window, chin up, huge hopeful smile. The couple looks around, genuinely charmed. The agent gestures at the view, completely deadpan, never acknowledging the costume man. No text, no logos.
```
