- **Format**: fake security-camera footage — the "caught on camera" genre that
  stops the scroll in frame 1 (voyeurism hook). The Thai recipe inside: absurd
  efficiency played dead straight, nobody acknowledges the weirdness.
- **TikTok mechanics**: HOOK = the surveillance angle itself (0.5 s recognition) ·
  LOOP = she exits toward where she entered, so the clip re-feeds itself ·
  COMMENT BAIT = "is this real?" + agents tagging each other · SOUND = designed

- **Heroine**: P'Fon — attach face refs (she is the only woman in frame; the two
  client couples: describe stays generic, faces small at this distance).
- **Model**: Seedance 2.0 · 9:16 · 8 s · single continuous shot

  prompt — the model hallucinates timestamps and REC icons (same failure as the

  dressing (timestamp, camera label, slight scanlines, speed-ramp) is post.
- **Post**: corner timestamp + "CAM 04 — ชั้น 12" label overlay, 1.2× speed ramp,
  caption suggestion: **"กล้องหอจับภาพได้… เอเจนท์คนนี้ไม่ธรรมดา"** (*"dorm camera
  caught it… this agent is not normal"*), end card + logo on the last beat.
