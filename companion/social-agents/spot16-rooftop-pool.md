# Agent social spot 16 — "ขอลองก่อน" Testing the View (4K cinematic 16:9, 1×8 s)

- **The concept**: a notch sexier than Elevator Mode. After the last viewing of
  the day, P'Fon has the rooftop infinity pool of her own listing to herself —
  and tests the lifestyle she sells. Heels off, blazer slips off her shoulders,
  bare legs in the water, head tipped back against the golden skyline. The Thai
  wink that keeps it charming instead of gratuitous: **she is trying the product
  before the client** — super: **«ขายวิว… แต่ขอลองก่อนนะคะ»** (*"I sell the view…
  but let me test it first"*).
- **Sexy register**: sensual through skin-light, water and posture — silk
  camisole, bare legs, golden glow. Tasteful, never vulgar; the confidence is
  the heat.
- **Why 4K**: water droplets flying in the splash kick, skin glow at blue hour,
  skyline bokeh across the infinity edge.
- **Generation safety (one-shot design)**: ONE person, static camera, seated at
  the pool edge (no swimming, no diving — no full-body water physics), simple
  sequence: sit, blazer off shoulders, feet dip, one small splash kick, hair
  toss, over-the-shoulder smile. No speech.
- **Heroine**: P'Fon — attach 2–3 face reference frames.
- **Model**: Seedance 2.0 · **16:9** · 4K · 8 s · single continuous shot
- **Post**: warm sunset R&B; supers on the kick; end card **«นิวาส — ขายไลฟ์สไตล์
  ต้องรู้จักไลฟ์สไตล์»** (*"to sell a lifestyle, know the lifestyle"*) + logo.
  Caption bait: «งานนี้มีข้อดีนะ» (*"this job has its perks"*).
