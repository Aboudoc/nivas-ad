# Agent social spot 13 — "ดีลแรก" The First Deal (4K flagship, 2×8 s)

## Why this is the buzz shot

The register that made Thai advertising world-famous is the tearjerker — and the
identity that unites ALL Thai 20–30s (not just agents) is the **hustle**: the side
gigs, the rain, the months before the first win. This spot is 16 seconds of that
life: night of doubt → morning of the first closed deal. It reaches far beyond
agents (every young freelancer shares it, comments it, tags a friend), while
recruiting the next generation of agents. Emotion travels deeper than comedy —
this is the flagship.

**Comment-bait caption**: «เล่าดีลแรกของคุณให้ฟังหน่อย» (*"tell us about your first
deal"*) — the comments write the campaign.

## The 5-generation budget plan

Only 5 free 4K generations. Order: **clip 1 → clip 2 → spot 14 (1 clip)** = 3
generations, keeping **2 spare** for one retake each of whichever clip needs it.
Both prompts below are built maximum-safety: ONE person in frame, static camera,
simple sequential gestures, zero speech, zero text-bearing props, clean-frame
block. 4K is exploited through texture: steam, rain bokeh, fabric, glistening
eyes.

- **Heroine**: P'Fon — attach 2–3 face reference frames to BOTH clips (she is the
  only person in frame; the vendor is never shown).
- **Model**: Seedance 2.0 · 9:16 · 4K · 8 s · single continuous shot each
- **Post**: piano score, no VO. Supers: clip 1 → **«3 เดือน… ยังไม่ปิดดีลแรก»**
  (*"3 months… still no first deal"*); clip 2 → **«จนกระทั่งเช้านี้»** (*"until this
  morning"*). End card: **«นิวาส — เคียงข้างทุกดีลแรก»** (*"Nivas — beside every
  first deal"*) + logo, 2 s of quiet.

## Clip 1 — the night (4K texture: rain, steam, neon)

```
Vertical 9:16, single continuous 8-second shot, static camera at counter height, photorealistic, naturalistic muted warm grade like an emotional Thai film, razor-sharp fine detail. CHARACTER REFERENCE: the woman is the same as in the attached photos; she is the ONLY person in frame.

Late night inside a small Bangkok noodle stall. A young Thai woman in a slightly rain-damp light blazer sits alone at the counter by the window; heavy rain streams down the glass behind her, blurred neon signs glowing through the wet window as soft bokeh. A motorcycle helmet rests on the counter beside an untouched bowl of noodles, steam rising slowly through the warm lamp light. She looks at the smartphone in her hands (screen angled away from the camera), then gently places it face-down on the counter, exhales a long tired breath, and rests her chin on her hand, eyes down — tired but not broken. Fine detail on the rising steam, the rain droplets on the glass, the weave of her blazer.

No one speaks on camera. Only one person in frame. ABSOLUTELY CLEAN FRAME: no watermark, no icons, no overlay, no on-screen text, no readable signs or labels anywhere. Plain unedited camera footage.
```

## Clip 2 — the morning (4K texture: golden light, glistening eyes)

```
Vertical 9:16, single continuous 8-second shot, identical static camera at counter height, the same small Bangkok noodle stall now in soft golden morning light through the clean window, photorealistic, naturalistic warm grade, razor-sharp fine detail. CHARACTER REFERENCE: the woman is the same as in the attached photos; she is the ONLY person in frame.

The same young Thai woman sits at the same counter seat, the motorcycle helmet still beside her. Her smartphone lies face-down; she picks it up, turns it over and reads (screen angled away from the camera). She goes completely still — then her hand rises slowly to her mouth, her eyes glisten with tears, and a laugh breaks through at the same time, joy and disbelief in one expression. She takes one breath, straightens her back, picks up the helmet and hugs it to her chest with a small determined smile, morning light warm on her face. Fine detail on the glistening eyes, the golden light, the fabric.
```
