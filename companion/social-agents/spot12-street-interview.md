# Agent social spot 12 — street micro-interview (Real Thai / TikTok, 3×8 s)

- **Format**: the viral vox-pop — a mic enters the frame, one question, escalating
  answers. THE most stitchable format on Thai TikTok. Question (post overlay, not
  spoken on camera): **"มีกี่ห้องในมือครับ?"** (*"how many units do you manage?"*).
- **TikTok mechanics**: HOOK = the mic in frame 1 (instant genre recognition) ·
  ESCALATION = binder man → notebook auntie → P'Fon's silent phone · COMMENT/DUET
  BAIT = agents will stitch with their own answer — that IS the campaign ·
  LOOP = P'Fon's exit walks toward where clip A starts.
- **The interviewer is never seen**: only a hand holding a plain black microphone
  (no logo cube, no foam label) enters from frame edge. One character per clip.
- **⚠️ Face-ref rule**: attach P'Fon's refs ONLY to clip C. Clips A and B get NO
  reference images — their characters are described explicitly different.
- **Model**: Seedance 2.0 · 9:16 · 3 clips × 8 s, single continuous shot each
- **Grade**: bright Thai street daylight, punchy TVC colors, slight film grain —
  same corner under the BTS station in all three clips (continuity).
- **Post**: question text overlay top of frame on each clip, burned-in Thai
  subtitles, end card after clip C: **"นิวาส คอมแพเนียน — คำตอบอยู่ในมือ"**
  (*"the answer is in your hand"*) + logo + QR.
