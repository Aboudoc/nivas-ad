# Agent social spot 15 — "โหมดลิฟต์" Elevator Mode (4K cinematic 16:9, 1×8 s)

- **The concept**: the Thai-sexy register done cinematic — the **elevator
  transformation**. Alone in the lift between two viewings, P'Fon drops the
  professional mask: hair comes down in slow motion, shoulders roll, a glamour
  gaze straight into the lens… then the floor light blinks — and she SNAPS back
  to hyper-professional mode in half a second, posture, smile, wai toward the
  doors. Sensual in private, impeccable in public: the duality every 20–30
  working woman knows. The instant switch IS the punchline (Thai sexy = the
  comedy carries the heat, never vulgar).
- **Buzz mechanics**: meme framing **«โหมดตัวเอง / โหมดลูกค้า»** (*"own mode /
  client mode"*) — screenshot-able before/after; caption bait: **«โหมดลิฟต์
  ใครเป็นบ้าง»** (*"elevator mode — who else does this?"*).
- **Why 4K + 16:9**: pure texture cinema — hair strands falling in slow motion,
  silk blouse sheen, brushed-steel reflections, warm light strip. Widescreen makes
  it a film, not a phone clip.
- **Generation safety (one-shot design)**: ONE person, static camera, elevator
  framed on its BACK wall (no doors visible, no door movement — the arrival is a
  light cue), simple sequential gestures (pull hair tie, hair falls, shoulder
  roll, gaze, snap posture, wai), no speech, blank surfaces.
- **Heroine**: P'Fon — attach 2–3 face reference frames.
- **Model**: Seedance 2.0 · **16:9** · 4K · 8 s · single continuous shot
- **Post**: sultry slow track that cuts DEAD on the light blink, one elevator
  "ding!", then silence on the wai. Supers: «โหมดตัวเอง» on the hair fall,
  «โหมดลูกค้า» on the snap. End card: **«นิวาส — ความมั่นใจของเอเจนท์»** (*"Nivas —
  the agent's confidence"*) + logo.
