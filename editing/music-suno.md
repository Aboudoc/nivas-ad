# Music generation — Suno (spot soundtrack)

The music track for the spots is generated on **Suno** (free daily credits; a
paid plan is required for commercial-use rights before publishing as an ad).

## Settings (every run)

- **Instrumental: ON** · **Lyrics field: EMPTY** (anything in Lyrics gets SUNG)
- The description goes in the **Style** field only
- Each run produces 2 versions — generate 2 runs, pick the take with the
  clearest part-1/part-2 contrast

## Lessons from real runs

- Instrument palette IS the genre: `ukulele + marimba + claps` = mobile-game
  jingle (rejected); western funk/brass = generic (rejected).
- **The buzz sound for Thai 20–30 = modern Isaan fusion (molam trap)**: phin
  lute riff + khaen + luk thung 3-cha groove over 808s/trap drums — the
  MILLI/F.HERO viral recipe: instantly Thai to a Thai ear, modern, comedy-friendly.
- Suno follows a described 3-part ARC but NOT precise timestamps. Writing
  "(0-8s)" is worth trying but unreliable — the deterministic method is cutting
  the track at the part boundaries in CapCut and aligning each section to its
  clip (three audio pieces instead of one; cut on a strong beat, inaudible
  under the VO).

## SELECTED prompt — spot 1 "Day-in-the-life" (3×8 s structure)

```
modern Thai molam trap fusion, catchy phin lute riff hook, khaen accents, bouncy 808 bass, crisp trap drums, luk thung 3-cha groove, instrumental. Three-part structure, each part exactly 8 seconds: PART 1 (0-8s) fast frantic chaotic energy, busy racing phin riff over hectic double-time drums; PART 2 (8-16s) sudden stripped-down chill, sparse warm phin notes over a slow confident 808 groove, smooth and effortless; PART 3 (16-24s) the full groove returns bigger and happier, festive 3-cha party feel, building to one clean punchy final hit, hard stop at 24 seconds, no fade-out.
```

## Mapping to the edit (CapCut)

- PART 1 = clip 1 motosai chaos (racing phin + hectic drums)
- PART 2 = clip 2 lobby (the beat stripping down IS the calm gag)
- PART 3 = clip 3 key handover (festive return); the **final hit lands on the
  wink**, then the end card stays silent
- Music bed at ~20% under the VO (auto-ducking or manual volume)
